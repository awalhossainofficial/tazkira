# Zikr

A Mac menu-bar app that gently reminds the user to remember Allah through the day by showing a short remembrance at the top of the screen.

## Language

### What is shown

**Remembrance**:
One piece of text to recite: a Zikr, a Dua or a Durood, shown in Arabic, then in English letters (its Transliteration), then with a short English meaning.
_Avoid_: Item, quote, message, card

**Zikr**:
A short phrase glorifying or praising Allah, such as SubhanAllah or Alhamdulillah.
_Avoid_: Dhikr, tasbih (as a category name)

**Dua**:
A short supplication asking Allah for something.
_Avoid_: Prayer (that word means salah)

**Durood**:
A salutation sending blessings on the Prophet ﷺ.
_Avoid_: Darud, Salawat

**Built-in List**:
The fixed set of Remembrances that ships with the app; the user can switch each one on or off but cannot add their own.
_Avoid_: Library, catalogue, collection

### When and how it is shown

**Reminder**:
One appearance of a Remembrance on screen.
_Avoid_: Notification, alert, popup

**Pill**:
The small black shape at the top centre of the screen where a Reminder appears, on every Mac whether or not it has a notch.
_Avoid_: Dynamic Island, notch, banner

**Pill Style**:
One visual design of the Pill: Island, Wings, Drop Card or Peek.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, layout

**Interval**:
How many minutes pass between one Reminder and the next, chosen by the user.
_Avoid_: Timer, frequency, mode

**Round**:
One pass through every switched-on Remembrance in shuffled order; no Remembrance repeats until the Round is finished.
_Avoid_: Cycle, loop

**Pause**:
A user-chosen stretch of time (one hour, or until 6:00 am the next morning) during which no Reminders appear.
_Avoid_: Snooze, mute, do not disturb
